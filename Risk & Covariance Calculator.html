2. Results from scenarios

Expected return = Σ p × R. Variance = Σ p × (R − E[R])². Covariance(A,B) = Σ p × (RA − E[RA]) × (RB − E[RB]). Beta = Cov(stock, market) ÷ Variance(market).

3. Quick calculator: already know the market's standard deviation?

Covariance = correlation × stock SD × market SD. Beta = covariance ÷ market variance.
